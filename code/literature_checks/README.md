# literature_checks (25 Oct 2026)

Scripts and outputs produced when the papers folder was reviewed (see docs/15_Shape_Ripple_FSS_Checks_25Oct.md).

sim2e.py is sim2.py with two added contact modes (3: oblate or prolate spheroid, GJK with an analytic support function; 4: zero-thickness convex polygon, exact line-interval test); nothing else in the simulator is changed, and regress_sim2e.py (with sim2.py beside it) confirms identical first-wrap counts for modes 0, 1 and 2. Copy it next to numba and numpy and run the others from the same folder; finallaw3.py and mylaw.py must be on the path for experimental_comparison.py (they are in the parent code/ folder).

ell_test.py: one threshold run (mode, aspect ratio, box, realisations). ell_batch.py with ell_batch.json and ell_batch.log: oblate spheroids at aspect ratios 2 to 2000 in boxes of 24 and 36 radii, with the ratio to Garboczi et al. 1995 Table I (occupied fractions). prolate_test.py: prolate spheroids at aspect ratios 2 to 20 against Yi and Sastry 2004 Table 4. garboczi_orient_test.py: thin spheroids with the polar angle drawn uniformly in theta, the orientation-sampling hypothesis. experimental_comparison.py and .csv: the 16 experimental cases against the law (measured over predicted).

Conventions: lengths in units of the equatorial radius; aspect ratio u = R over the polar semi-axis for spheroids and 2R/t for cylinders; eta = (4/3) pi n R^3; nominal volume fraction nV, occupied fraction 1 - exp(-nV). Garboczi's tabulated fractions are occupied fractions. The sphere extrapolation (boxes 10 to 56, exponent 1/nu = 1/0.8765) is sphere_fss.py (log sphere_fss.log) and gives 0.3386.

Added later on 25 Oct (results in docs/15_Shape_Ripple_FSS_Checks_25Oct.md): poly_test.py (excluded volume of squares, triangles and a 720-gon against A P / 2); shape_fss.py with run_shapes.sh and shape_fss.jsonl (disc, square, triangle in a periodic box, boxes 16 to 128); span_open.py with run_span.sh, run_span2.sh and span_open.jsonl (Yi and Tawerghi's open-face spanning procedure); ell_fss.py with run_ell.sh and ell_fss.jsonl (spheroid thin limit, aspect ratios 100 and 1000, boxes 24 to 96); prolate_fss.py and prolate_fss.jsonl (prolate aspect ratio 10, boxes 12 to 48 long semi-axes); ripple_mesh_test.py with ripple_mesh_test.json and .log (sim4.py, copied unchanged from code/, against an independent triangulated mesh); fss_analysis.py and fss_analysis.txt (summary of the jsonl files); regress_sim2e.py. For shapes other than discs the radius is the equivalent radius (the circle of the same area), as in Yi and Tawerghi, so eta = (4/3) pi n r^3 is comparable across shapes. The jsonl files are append-only logs; each line is one run with its seed. shape_fss.jsonl and span_open.jsonl were started from empty files on 25 Oct.

Added 25 Oct (evening): spheroid_vex_test.py (log spheroid_vex_test.log) compares the spheroid contact test with the excluded volume of the spheroid (2V + S M / 2 pi, Kihara) for c/a of 1, 1/3, 0.1 and 0.01; ratios 1.0001, 1.0001, 0.9991 and 0.9995.

How to run these scripts in the review folder: they import sim2.py, sim4.py, sim2e.py, finallaw3.py and mylaw.py by module name. Copy literature_checks/ and the Python files of code/ into one folder under review_work/ (sim2e.py, sim4.py and the scripts are in literature_checks/; sim2.py, finallaw3.py and mylaw.py are in code/) and run from there. The gap_tests/ folder holds the pre-registered tests; copy its files to the same place.
